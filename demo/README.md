# Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness shareware

`war2sw108.zip` is Blizzard Entertainment's Warcraft II shareware CD, version 1.08 (January 1996), unmodified.
It came from the Internet Archive (item `WarcraftIiTidesOfDarknessDemo`).
SHA-256: `bab9f43fd30f8c27c10eb72729961b5df1142d12732e6049f51bf6c7bba48bb0`

free of charge, complete and unmodified. Bundling it with other software needs Blizzard's prior written permission;
Jon (the project's author) asked Blizzard and was told on 15 November 2026 that including it here is fine.

The game reads `demodata/maindat.war`, `rezdat.war` and `sfxdat.sud` straight out of the zip on first launch,
so anyone can play the demo battlefield without owning Warcraft II. Nothing in the zip is changed or repackaged.
